About Hannah

Hi, I'm Hannah King — the heart and hands behind Petal and Pearl Flowers.

As a student florist at Kansas State University, I fell in love with the way a single bloom can hold a memory. Whether it's the joy of a Tuesday surprise bouquet or the once-in-a-lifetime magic of a wedding day, I believe flowers have a way of anchoring us right where we are — fully present and deeply grateful.

Every arrangement I create is designed with intention, celebrating the organic beauty of each season and the unique story you're telling. From garden-gathered textures to soft, romantic palettes, my work draws on classic elegance with a touch of warmth that feels like home.

My passion is capturing special moments through florals that remind us to live in the present.
